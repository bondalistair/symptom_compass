Why this feels confusing

Dozens of therapy names. No clear way to choose.

CBT. DBT. Psychodynamic. EMDR. Person-centered. Search "what type of therapy do I need" and you'll find long lists of approaches — with no clear way to tell which one fits what you're actually going through.

Symptom Compass starts from the other direction. Instead of a list of therapy names, you start from how you're feeling, and we point you toward the approaches that commonly help with that — a starting point to bring into your first conversation with a professional.

Questions

Good to know

Is this the same as therapy?

No. Symptom Compass helps you understand what kind of therapy might fit your situation — it doesn't replace working with a licensed therapist.

How accurate is it?

It's a starting point based on common patterns between symptoms and therapy approaches, not a clinical assessment. Think of it as a compass, not a map.

Do I need to create an account?

No — it's free to use, with no sign-up required.

What if I don't know how to describe what I'm feeling?

That's completely normal. The tool is built to work with plain, everyday language — you don't need clinical terms.
