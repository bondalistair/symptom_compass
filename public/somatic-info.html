What is Somatic Therapy?

Somatic Therapies — body-oriented approaches that work with physical sensations and the nervous system, alongside talking.

The basic idea

Somatic therapies (such as Somatic Experiencing or sensorimotor psychotherapy) start from the idea that stress and trauma aren't only held in our thoughts — they're held in the body too, in patterns of tension, bracing, or nervous system activation. These approaches work directly with bodily sensations, not just conversation.

What a course usually looks like

Sessions might involve tracking physical sensations as they arise, gentle movement, breathing techniques, and grounding exercises, often woven together with more traditional talking. The pace is usually kept slow and titrated to avoid overwhelming the nervous system.

What it's commonly used for

Somatic approaches are most often used for trauma and chronic stress, particularly where physical symptoms of anxiety or hypervigilance haven't fully resolved through talking-based therapy alone.

What to expect from an initial assessment

An initial conversation usually covers your history, any physical symptoms you notice (tension, restlessness, being easily startled), and how comfortable you feel with a more body-focused style of work.
